Spatius adds a real-time avatar layer to voice AI applications. Bring your own voice stack,
stream synchronized motion, and render the avatar on the user's device.

**[Try the live demo](https://www.spatius.ai/playground/)** · [Website](https://www.spatius.ai) · [Docs](https://docs.spatius.ai)

Our Spatius plugin is available in LiveKit Agents, with an [integration guide in LiveKit's documentation](https://docs.livekit.io/agents/models/avatar/plugins/spatius/).
